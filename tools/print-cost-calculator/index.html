How the math works (so you can trust it)

Material = spool price ÷ spool weight × grams used.
Electricity = power (W) ÷ 1000 × print hours × $/kWh.
Depreciation = printer cost ÷ expected life-hours × print hours.
Labor = hands-on minutes ÷ 60 × your hourly rate.
Failure allowance = (material + electricity + depreciation) × failure rate %. (You still pay for filament and power on failed prints, so a share of them is baked into every good unit.)
True cost = material + electricity + depreciation + labor + failure allowance + packaging.
Suggested price is solved so that, after marketplace + payment fees, your profit equals your target margin of the price: price = (cost + feeFlat) ÷ (1 − marginRate − feePctRate − payPctRate).
That last step is what generic calculators skip — it's why sellers who "add 50%" still lose money once Etsy takes its cut.
