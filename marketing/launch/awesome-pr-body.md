Adds **PrintProfit Calculator** to *Online Tools*.

- Free, browser-only pricing calculator for people who *sell* 3D prints. Where most cost calculators stop at filament + electricity, this one adds hands-on labor, printer depreciation, a failure/reprint allowance, packaging, and marketplace + payment fees (Etsy, Etsy Offsite Ads, eBay, Stripe presets), then solves the listing price backwards from a target margin.
- No signup, no upload — the math runs client-side and a "Copy shareable link" encodes the inputs in the URL fragment.
- Single static HTML page, source in the public repo it is served from.
