## Full description

**Most 3D-print sellers price like this: filament cost × 3. Then Etsy takes its cut, a print fails, packaging eats a dollar — and the "profitable" listing quietly loses money.**

PrintProfit Pro fixes the math. It's a clean, editable spreadsheet (works in **Excel, Google Sheets, and LibreOffice**) that prices your prints on what they *actually* cost you:

- ✅ **Filament** — by the gram, from your real spool price
- ✅ **Electricity** — from your printer's wattage and your kWh rate
- ✅ **Machine depreciation** — your printer wears out; this charges a fair share per print hour
- ✅ **Your labor** — setup, removal, finishing, at a rate you set
- ✅ **Failure / reprint allowance** — you still pay for filament and power on failed prints
- ✅ **Packaging & consumables**
- ✅ **Marketplace + payment fees** — Etsy, Stripe, eBay presets included

Then it does the one thing hobby math gets wrong: it **works the price backwards** so your target profit margin is what's left *after* the fees — not before them.

### What's inside
- **Settings tab** — enter your machine, power rate, and labor rate once.
- **Pricing tab** — up to 40 products; type in the green cells, everything else calculates. Get a fee-aware suggested price and true margin per product.
- **Monthly P&L tab** — punch in units sold and see real revenue, cost, and take-home.
- **Start Here tab** — plain-English instructions.

### Who it's for
Hobbyists turning prints into a side income on Etsy, Facebook, craft fairs, or their own site — who want to know they're actually making money before they scale up.

### Honest note
This is a tool to help you price with your eyes open. It does **not** guarantee sales or income, and it can't — real costs vary (supports, shipping, returns, taxes). Treat suggested prices as a floor to build from, not a promise.
